## Publication Policy

```text
Chinese article exists
!= English edition required

English edition exists
=> exact source artifact required

English essays
= GitHub-native Markdown

Chinese PDF / Markdown format
!= English format obligation
```

The Chinese repository is the complete body of work. This repository publishes a small, stable English selection with its own editorial structure and release cadence.

The two publication surfaces share provenance. They do not require symmetrical directories, identical formats, or simultaneous releases. A Chinese-only article creates no translation debt. Only an article explicitly selected above is pending English publication.

## Legacy Compatibility Routes

Some older Markdown paths remain as thin routes so established links do not break. They are not English publications, do not appear in the published index, and create no synchronization obligation. Future Chinese-only articles will not receive English placeholders by default.
